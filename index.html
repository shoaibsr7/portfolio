about

From PGA 2K25 to ocean science.

I'm a software developer with a Bachelor of Computer Science (Co-op) from Dalhousie University. Today I'm a Computer Systems Developer and Programmer at Fisheries and Oceans Canada. I build and maintain the web apps scientists and researchers use, including the public meetings and publications portals for the Canadian Science Advisory Secretariat.

My co-op terms took me all over the stack. I shipped gameplay features for PGA 2K25 at 2K Games. I automated AWS infrastructure at InnovaSea. I also built two educational games that are now on the App Store and Google Play.

I'm happiest working anywhere from pixel-level UI to cloud plumbing, and I care about software that's accessible, reliable and easy to use.
